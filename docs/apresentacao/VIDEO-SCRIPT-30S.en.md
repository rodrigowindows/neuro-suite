# Video Recording Script — CrewVitals (30 Seconds)

Portuguese version: [ROTEIRO-VIDEO-30S.md](ROTEIRO-VIDEO-30S.md)

## 1. Technical Recording Parameters

- Maximum duration: 30 seconds (strictly timed).
- Capture resolution: 1280x720 (720p) or 1920x1080 (1080p), 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Recording environment: Incognito browser window, bookmarks bar hidden, browser extensions disabled, system notifications muted, personal tabs closed.
- Entry point: `https://neuro-suite.lovable.app/demo?lang=en` (direct public access, no login and no registration).
- Audio / Voiceover: Objective, steady, and professional tone.
- Scientific honesty: Demonstration data are fixed simulated values; the predictive alert is a demonstration and not a clinically validated prediction model.
- Available interactive buttons from `Demo.tsx`: 'Start 4-7-8 Protocol', 'Simulate new scan', 'Start Shift', 'Restart demo'.
- No emojis used in titles, descriptions, or supporting materials.

## 3. Pre-Recording Checklist

1. Open browser in incognito mode and pre-load `https://neuro-suite.lovable.app/demo?lang=en`.
2. Verify that the 'DEMO: simulated data' seal is visible at the top and initial status is NO-GO (BioScore 34).
3. Test button interactions for 'Start 4-7-8 Protocol', 'Simulate new scan', 'Start Shift', and 'Restart demo' prior to recording.
4. Set browser zoom to 100% at 1280x720 or 1920x1080 resolution.
5. Start screen recorder and conduct a rehearsal with a stopwatch to confirm total time between 28 and 30 seconds.
6. After recording, inspect the resulting video file to verify that no personal information, background tabs, or credentials appear.

## 4. Subtitles and Accessibility

- If the video is recorded with English voiceover, Portuguese subtitles must strictly follow the narration text from [ROTEIRO-VIDEO-30S.md](ROTEIRO-VIDEO-30S.md) without loose translation or marketing embellishments.
- If the video is recorded with Portuguese voiceover, English subtitles must strictly match the voiceover text defined in this script.
- Synchronization: short subtitles (maximum 2 lines per screen, 32 characters per line) synchronized directly with scene timestamp intervals (00:00, 00:05, 00:12, 00:19, 00:27).
